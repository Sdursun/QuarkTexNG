Warp3D.h and Warp3D.fd are not in this archive
==============================================

Two files of this directory are missing from the source in the Aminet
archive: Warp3D.h and Warp3D.fd, the include files of the Warp3D API,
(C) 1998 Sam Jordan, Hans-Joerg Frieden, Thomas Frieden, all rights
reserved. They are not under QuarkTex NG's license, and their terms are not
known to allow passing them on, so they are left out here.

To build QuarkTex NG, put them into this directory (Warp3D.library/). They
are used unchanged, as they are in QuarkTex's original repository:

  https://github.com/RobDangerous/QuarkTex
    Warp3D.library/Warp3D.h
    Warp3D.library/Warp3D.fd

They are also part of the Warp3D developer files.
